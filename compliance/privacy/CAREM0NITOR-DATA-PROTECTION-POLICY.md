## Purpose

This policy explains how ANIKA IT SOLUTIONS LIMITED protects personal data processed through Caremonitor. It applies to employees, contractors, administrators, care-provider customers, support personnel and suppliers with access to Caremonitor information.

## Roles and scope

Each care-provider customer normally decides why and how its client, carer and care records are processed and is therefore the data controller. ANIKA IT SOLUTIONS LIMITED normally acts as processor when it hosts and supports Caremonitor for that customer. This must be confirmed in each customer contract and data-processing agreement (DPA). ANIKA IT SOLUTIONS LIMITED may be an independent controller for its own staff, commercial contacts, billing, security logs and service administration.

## Core requirements

1. Process personal data only for a documented, lawful care, employment, contractual, security or legal purpose.
2. Use the minimum data required and keep it accurate.
3. Apply role-based access, organisation separation and individual accounts.
4. Use MFA for privileged, production-support and remotely accessible accounts.
5. Use approved suppliers only, with a DPA where they process personal data.
6. Protect data in transit with HTTPS/TLS and protect secrets in the production environment.
7. Retain information only under an approved retention schedule and legal-hold process.
8. Report suspected personal-data incidents immediately through the incident process.
9. Assess material changes, new integrations and high-risk processing before release.
10. Train staff annually and remove access promptly when roles end.

## Individual rights

Requests for access, correction, restriction, objection, deletion or portability must be recorded, verified, assessed against legal/clinical retention obligations and answered within applicable deadlines. Caremonitor contains controlled workflows for export, correction, erasure and legal holds; customer controllers make final decisions about client-care records.

## Incident management

Staff must report a suspected loss, inappropriate access, phishing incident, system compromise or data disclosure immediately to the service owner and DPO/IG Lead. The incident lead records facts, contains the issue, preserves evidence, assesses impact, notifies affected customers and regulators where required, and records lessons learned. Where required, personal-data breaches are reported through the DSPT/ICO route within 72 hours of discovery.

## Governance and review

The SIRO, DPO/IG Lead and technical service owner review this policy annually, after material changes or incidents. Exceptions require written approval, an expiry date and a compensating control.
