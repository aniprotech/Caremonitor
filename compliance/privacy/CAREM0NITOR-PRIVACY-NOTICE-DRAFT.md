## Who this notice covers

This notice explains how Caremonitor handles personal information about care clients, family or authorised contacts, care workers, employees and business contacts. Your care provider remains the main contact for information in your care record.

## Information processed

Caremonitor may process identity and contact details; care, support-plan, visit, medication, risk, allergy and communication information; staff employment, training and availability information; financial records; documents; service communications; audit logs; and technical security data such as session/device information.

## Why information is used

Information is used to deliver, coordinate and evidence care; schedule visits and tasks; manage medication; communicate with authorised people; administer staff and finance processes; protect the service; meet legal obligations; and improve service quality. Caremonitor must not be used for unrelated marketing of care-record information.

## Legal basis and special-category information

The applicable customer care provider decides the lawful basis for care-record processing and will explain it to you. Health and care information is special-category personal data and requires an additional condition under UK data-protection law. ANIKA IT SOLUTIONS LIMITED processes customer care data only under documented customer instructions, except where law requires otherwise.

## Who receives information

Access is restricted to authorised staff of the relevant care organisation and authorised people selected by that organisation. Approved suppliers may process limited information to host, secure, send communications or support the service. Suppliers must be assessed and subject to appropriate contractual protections.

## Retention and security

Records are retained according to the customer organisation’s approved retention schedule, legal obligations and any legal hold. Caremonitor uses individual accounts, role-based access, audit records and encrypted transport. No system can guarantee absolute security; suspected incidents are investigated and managed under the incident process.

## Your rights and how to complain

You may ask the relevant care provider about access, correction, restriction, objection, deletion or portability. Requests may be limited where health-care, safeguarding, legal, financial or record-retention obligations apply. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Contact `[insert DPO/privacy contact]` first so we can address the issue.
