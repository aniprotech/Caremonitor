## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure covers Caremonitor staff onboarding, training and competency checks, DBS/right-to-work records, periodic access review and leaver actions. It applies to carers, managers, administrators, support staff and technical administrators who can access live Caremonitor data or supporting services.

The organisation, not the software, must verify original documents, perform employment checks, make suitability decisions and retain the lawful HR evidence. Do not mark an individual compliant merely because a file was uploaded.

## 3. Worker onboarding and readiness process

1. Confirm the worker's identity, role, employment/engagement basis and right to work in accordance with the organisation's HR process.
2. Record DBS status where appropriate to the role and safeguarding risk, including reference, check date and renewal/recheck date. Do not store excess disclosure content.
3. Record references, contract, required professional registration and role-specific competencies.
4. Upload only necessary evidence to the restricted onboarding record. Avoid duplicating data in email or personal drives.
5. Configure mandatory courses in Governance and set `blocks roster` for tasks that must not be assigned until competency is approved.
6. A named manager reviews each required item and records the decision. Only then may the worker be marked ready or rostered.
7. Review expiring records at least monthly and before any lapse; withdraw or limit assignments where safeguards require it.

The Workforce Lead must tailor requirements to actual services. The following are a starting point, not a legal determination.

| Requirement | Mandatory | Typical renewal | Blocks rostering | Evidence / reviewer |

| Medication support | Only where worker administers/supports medication | 12-36 months per policy | Yes for medication tasks | Certificate and competency / clinical lead |

| First aid | Where operational risk assessment requires it | Per certificate | Role dependent | Certificate / manager |
| Role-specific communication or accessible-information support | Where client assessment requires it | Per care plan | Yes for affected assignment | Competency / care manager |

## 6. Leaver and urgent access-removal process

For a leaver, suspension or compromised account, a manager must notify the Technical Service Owner immediately. The owner must remove or disable Caremonitor access and revoke supplier/service access proportionately to the risk. Preserve records necessary for investigation and legal retention; do not destroy evidence.

| Action | Owner | Target | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Confirm last working/access date and access scope | Line manager | Before departure | HR/manager confirmation |
| Deactivate Caremonitor account and check sessions | Caremonitor administrator | Same day; immediately for risk | Audit entry / screenshot reference |
| Remove Railway, GitHub, email, Expo and NHS platform access as applicable | Relevant system owner | Same day; immediately for risk | Supplier access record |
| Reassign open visits, tasks and inbox responsibilities | Operations manager | Before or at departure | Assignment record |
| Recover equipment and apply device wipe/MDM process | IT owner | Per device policy | Asset-register record |
| Perform post-leaver access review | Technical Service Owner | Within five working days | Access-review record |
